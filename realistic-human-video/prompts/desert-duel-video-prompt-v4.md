TITLE: "FASHION DUEL" — a 24-second vertical (9:16) photorealistic action-comedy short film,
directed like a modern Hollywood action movie.

LOGLINE
In a sunbaked desert, a young man and an elderly woman face off in a Western-style gun duel.
The bullets don't hurt — whoever gets hit is instantly transformed into a brand-new,
head-to-toe fashionable outfit. It's a battle of who can make the other look cooler.
The grandmother wins: the young man's new look is so impossibly cool that he admits defeat
and faints face-first into the sand, while she blows the smoke off her revolver at the camera.

CHARACTERS — FACE LOCK: the faces must match the reference people EXACTLY in every shot,
including extreme close-ups, profiles and fast motion. Never beautify, age, reshape or swap
the faces.
- THE YOUNG MAN = <<<f264355d-52eb-4665-9e9c-a58c36c62a47>>>. Always screen-LEFT, facing RIGHT.
  Before: his original horizontal-striped Breton shirt look.
  After (exactly the black outfit of the young man in the attached image of him in black):
  long, sharply tailored black leather trench coat worn open, black high-neck knit, black
  wide-leg pleated trousers, polished black lace-up boots, layered silver chains and rings,
  slim black sunglasses — a runway-level look.
- THE GRANDMOTHER = <<<4f672ef6-b040-4eb9-b178-2cf69c5f931d>>>. Always screen-RIGHT, facing LEFT.
  Before: her original blue outfit.
  After (exactly the cream outfit of the grandmother in the attached image of her in cream):
  cream-white tailored long coat, cream silk blouse, cream wide-leg trousers, patterned silk
  scarf, gold jewelry, tortoiseshell sunglasses pushed up on her head, white leather sneakers.

WORLD & LOOK
- Vast desert with low dunes and scattered rocks; low late-afternoon sun from screen-LEFT,
  long shadows, drifting dust, an occasional tumbleweed.
- Anamorphic 35mm film look: oval bokeh, horizontal lens flares, warm amber highlights and
  teal shadows, subtle film grain, high contrast.
- Editing rhythm: hard cuts on the beat, speed ramps (slow motion snapping back into real
  time), whip pans, snap zooms, short camera shakes on every gunshot.

DIALOGUE (spoken in Japanese, exactly as written)
- SHOT 07, the young man, before he fires: 「その服時代遅れだよ」
- SHOT 14, the grandmother, before she fires: 「本当のおしゃれを教えてやるよ」
- SHOT 19, the young man, just before he falls: 「カッコ良すぎる...」

CONTINUITY RULES (every shot)
- They always face each other and look at each other — never at the camera, except in the
  final shot. In the two opening muzzle shots only the gun points at the lens; their eyes
  look at their opponent.
- Both always hold an antique Western prop revolver in their right hand.
- Never cross the 180-degree line: man screen-left, grandmother screen-right, even in
  over-the-shoulder shots.
- Gunfire looks real (muzzle flash, white smoke, recoil, a small brass bullet in flight), but
  every hit is completely harmless: no injury, no blood, no wound. On impact only the clothes
  change, spreading outward from the impact point like a wave, with a small puff of smoke.

  0.8–1.4s: a fast, smooth rack focus — the muzzle melts into a huge soft blur and his face

  CAMERA: 50mm, profile, locked-off. He looks down at himself, lifts the leather lapel with

  small, cool wink. The fallen man lies blurred in the background on the left. Freeze frame on
  the wink.

SOUND DESIGN
- Act 1: tense spaghetti-western whistle and twangy guitar, wind hiss, ticking silence; a

- Gunshots: sharp cinematic cracks with a long echo across the desert.
- Bullet cams: slowed-down whoosh with a deep bass hum.
- Transformations: a satisfying fabric swoosh plus a sparkling shimmer.
- Fall: a soft comedic thud into sand. Final wink: a single playful guitar sting.
