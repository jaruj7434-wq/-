## 1. Top and trending games and genres on Poki and CrazyGames; small-team hits

### Takeaway
Long-running casual hits still lead: Subway Surfers, Level Devil, Drive Mad, Retro Bowl, Blocky Blast Puzzle and MineFun.io on Poki, and multiplayer .io/competitive games such as Smash Karts and 1v1.lol on CrazyGames. Three trends recur in 2025–2026: obby/parkour games (a Roblox genre moving to HTML5), "Italian Brainrot" meme games, and sort/organize puzzles. Solo developers can succeed with many small, simple physics or arcade games (Blumgi is the clearest example).

### Cited Findings
- Poki's popular page lists Subway Surfers, and Steal a Brainrot appeared there (the cached snapshot is old) — [Poki Popular](https://poki.com/en/popular)
- Roundups from May to August 2026 repeatedly list Subway Surfers, Level Devil, Drive Mad (described as Poki's most popular car game), Retro Bowl, Blocky Blast Puzzle and MineFun.io as top Poki games — [thenews.com.pk Aug 2026](https://www.thenews.com.pk/latest/1410914-top-10-best-poki-games-of-august-2026-check-list-here); [thenews June 2026](https://www.thenews.com.pk/latest/1404417-best-poki-games-for-june-2026-check-list-here); [jang.com.pk July 2026](https://jang.com.pk/en/68265-top-poki-games-for-july-2026-what-should-you-play-news) (listicles, not official analytics)
- CrazyGames is known for multiplayer and competitive casual games; Smash Karts and 1v1.lol are among its most popular — [tech-insider comparison 2026](https://tech-insider.org/crazygames-vs-poki-vs-coolmath-games-2026/)
- An August 2026 trend report names three recurring trends: obby/parkour ("Roblox's obstacle-course genre spilling into HTML5"), the Italian Brainrot meme universe "still spawning … games weekly", and "a quiet surge in sort-and-organize puzzles" — [booboo.cc Aug 2026](https://booboo.cc/blog/new-browser-games-august-2026) (self-interested source)

- Solo developer example: Loïc Roger (Blumgi Games: Blumgi Slime, Blumgi Merge, Blumgi Bounce) reached about 100M players in 2 years. He came from animation. "My first game still makes money today", and revenue is seasonal — [Poki blog 2023](https://poki.com/blog/blumgi-my-journey-on-the-web-how-i-reached-100m-players-in-2-years-as-an-indie-game-developer); [Poki spotlight Apr 2026](https://poki.com/blog/developer-spotlight-blumgi-games-part-1); [Part 2 May 2026](https://poki.com/blog/developer-spotlight-blumgi-games-part-2)
- Small-studio example: Kuyi Mobile (Defold) published several games on Poki, with about 67M gameplays across 5 Poki games and about 200k plays a day by the end of the year — [Kuyi Substack](https://kuyimobile.substack.com/p/my-game-production-process-and-how); [Defold spotlight Feb 2026](https://defold.com/2026/02/17/Creator-Spotlight-Kuyi-Mobile/)

- Platform scale: Poki had 625M players in 2025, 1B gameplays in a month, 100M+ monthly players, and 65 staff — [digitalmedianet](https://digitalmedianet.com/poki-announces-milestone-of-625-million-players-without-raising-external-funding/amp/); [Poki Dutch Game Awards Dec 2025](https://poki.com/blog/poki-wins-dutch-game-awards-2025). CrazyGames had 35M MAU across about 4,000 games (early 2025), and later claims 50M+ monthly players, mostly in Tier 1 markets — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/crazygames-hits-35m-users-for-browser-games-and-launches-social-multiplayer-features/); [cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-crazygames)
- .io context: the genre dates from about 2015–2016 (Agar.io wave), and games like ZombsRoyale.io still have large audiences — [Wikipedia .io games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.io_games)

### Inferences
- For a beginner, the evidence supports: (a) short-session, single-player casual games with instant fun: physics or platformer "troll" games like Level Devil, one-tap arcade games, simple driving or physics games like Drive Mad, and block, sort or merge puzzles; (b) meme or obby skins on proven mechanics for short-term trend traffic. Real-time multiplayer .io games are popular, but they need servers and a large player base, which makes them a poor first project.
- Blumgi shows that a portfolio strategy (many small games, iterated on and kept on the portal for years) works better than one big game.

## 2. Poki: submission, selection, revenue share, exclusivity, metrics, guidance

### Takeaway
Poki is curated and runs on data gates. A game goes through a Player Fit Test (500 players, playtime only), then a Web Fit Test (CTR and conversion on real category pages, about 7 days), then Soft Release, then Global Release with about 2 weeks of promotion. Revenue is ad-based: 50/50 on traffic Poki sends, 100% to the developer on traffic the developer brings. Licensing or exclusive deals exist. The technical bar is strict: an initial download under 8 MB (ideally 5 MB) and loading in under 10 seconds.

### Cited Findings
- Player Fit Test: "500 players try your game and we measure how long they play". Healthy result: 3+ minutes average playtime, and 25%+ of plays longer than 3 minutes — [Poki: How testing works](https://developers.poki.com/guide/how-testing-works) [primary]
- Web Fit Test: the game appears on real category pages. Poki measures click-through rate, time on page and conversion to play against category averages, over about 7 days — [Poki: How testing works](https://developers.poki.com/guide/how-testing-works) [primary]
- Poki recommends 65%+ conversion to play before requesting review. The average Poki game reaches about 70% conversion and 6+ minutes of playtime. "Treat these numbers as a floor, not a finish line." Strong metrics never override the quality guidelines — [Poki: Release process](https://developers.poki.com/guide/release-process); [sdk.poki.com/releaseprocess](https://sdk.poki.com/releaseprocess) [primary]
- Soft Release tracks conversion, engagement, errors, monetization (target at least 1 ad per DAU) and player feedback. Global Release brings a significant push for about 2 weeks — [Poki: Release process](https://developers.poki.com/guide/release-process) [primary]
- The dashboard shows DAU (playing and non-playing), engagement (time per DAU), earnings, ad performance, feedback and errors — [Poki SDK overview](https://developers.poki.com/guide/sdk-overview) [primary]
- Revenue share: 100% of revenue from users who reach the game directly (bookmarks, search, social, community links), and 50/50 on users Poki sends — [portalready / Poki terms summary](https://portalready.world/portals/); [cinevva Poki guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-poki). Poki also offers several deal types, including licensing and exclusivity deals with upfront or ongoing payments — [Poki Deal Types](https://sdk.poki.com/deals) [primary, snippet only]; [Mobidictum interview](https://mobidictum.com/pokis-web-gaming-interview-michiel-van-amerongen)
- Poki says revenue for its top developers grew tenfold in five years, to between about $50,000 and $1M a year — [Dealroom / Poki PR](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/poki-hits-1b-monthly-plays-as-developer-first-model-boosts-top-studio-revenues-tenfold-1); [TFN](https://techfundingnews.com/browser-gaming-website-poki-won-big-at-the-dutch-game-awards-celebrating-hitting-1-billion-monthly-plays/)
- Technical: "Target an initial download size under 8 MB" — [Poki Requirements](https://developers.poki.com/guide/requirements-quality) [primary]. The engine guide says the initial download should not exceed 5 MB, with 8 MB total, and that players leave if loading takes over 10 seconds — [Poki: Choosing your web engine](https://developers.poki.com/guide/web-engine) [primary] (the two pages differ)
- On mobile, the game must cover the full screen in portrait, landscape or both. Poki recommends progressive loading: menu, tutorial and first stage first, the rest in the background — [Poki Requirements](https://sdk.poki.com/new-requirements.html) [primary]
- Content and player-safety rules apply because the audience is young — [Poki: Content & player safety](https://developers.poki.com/guide/content-player-safety) [primary]
- 2026 State of Web Gaming report (Atomik Research, 2,000 players and 400 developers in the US and UK, May 2026): 58% of players choose web games because they're free, 56% because they're easy to access, 62% have downloaded or bought a game after playing it on the web, and 53% of studios plan to port mobile games to the browser — [Poki report](https://poki.com/blog/state-of-web-gaming-report-2026); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/report-53-plan-to-port-mobile-games-to-browser-in-next-12-months/)
- A November 2026 Defold article covers good practices for web games on Poki — [Defold blog 2026-11-03](https://defold.com/2026/11/03/Good-practices-for-web-games-on-Poki/)

### Inferences
- The first gate measures playtime only, so a beginner's game needs a hook within seconds and enough content to hold 3+ minutes. Many short levels or an endless loop with progression work well for this.
- The 5–8 MB limit favors Phaser, Three.js, PixiJS and similar plain JS/TS games, which suits Claude Code. Unity is the hardest fit.

### Gaps
- No primary Poki page confirming the 50/50 vs 100% wording was retrieved; it is cited through secondary summaries. Payment threshold and timing were not found.

## 3. CrazyGames: Basic Launch vs Full Launch, revenue share, SDK, metrics, earnings

### Takeaway
New games get a Basic Launch: a limited audience for 7–21 days, SDK optional and **no monetization**. Games that meet benchmarks (average playtime, conversion to gameplay, retention; about 10+ minutes and 10–15% D1 are cited as strong) are invited to a Full Launch. Full Launch requires the SDK and a full QA review, and it unlocks the ad revenue share. CrazyGames is non-exclusive. The general revenue-share percentage is not published; the 2026 jam terms gave developers 60% of ads and 70% of IAP after recoup.

### Cited Findings
- Basic Launch: "Test your game on our platform with a limited audience for a temporary period of 7 to 21 days. The SDK is optional and monetization is not available." Full Launch: "Requires Full Implementation… including a Full QA review. Monetization is enabled and you start receiving revenue share." — [CrazyGames docs intro](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/intro/) [primary]
- Progression is based on average playtime, conversion to gameplay and retention. Multiplayer games, or games already published elsewhere and invited, may skip Basic Launch — [CrazyGames docs](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/intro/) [primary]
- Basic Launch ends once the game has been live at least 7 days and reached 500 plays, or after 21 days. Successful games often see 10+ minutes average playtime and 10–15% D1 retention. Slow loading or confusing onboarding hurts conversion — [CrazyGames Basic Launch Guide](https://docs.crazygames.com/resources/basic-launch-metrics/) [primary; the conversion benchmark value was not visible]
- Only ads served through the CrazyGames SDK are allowed — [CrazyGames FAQ](https://docs.crazygames.com/faq/) [primary]
- Technical: initial download of 50 MB or less (20 MB or less for mobile homepage eligibility), 250 MB total, and about 1,500 files maximum (per third parties). Initial download is measured up to the gameplayStart event. Unity games are disabled on iOS by default — [CrazyGames Technical requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/technical/) [primary]; [cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-crazygames)
- Revenue share: no percentage in the general terms. Compensation is calculated monthly from users and ad performance (Terms 5.4). It applies only to Full Launch games with a current SDK, no other ads and no other portal's branding. Steam or app-store versions are fine — [portalready CrazyGames](https://portalready.world/portals/crazygames/); [Developer terms 2025-08-18 PDF](https://files.crazygames.com/documents/developer_terms_20250818.pdf)
- CrazyGames x GameMaker Web Jam (2026) terms: 60% of ad revenue and 70% of IAP to the developer, after recoup. IAP runs through Xsolla, by invitation, for high performers — [Jam guide](https://crazygames.indiehero.io/cggmwj-jg/); [cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-crazygames)
- Payout of €100 a month or more, reported by secondary sources only (unverified) — [cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-crazygames)
- CrazyGames says it pays "millions of euro" a year to developers — [Google AdSense case study](https://www.google.com/ads/publisher/stories/crazy_games/)
- A Claude Code example: a developer launched "Bolt Pit", a Three.js arena shooter built with Claude Code, on CrazyGames. It earned $0 at first because Basic Launch has no ads. They estimate $15,000 takes 1–15M plays — [X post @froessell](https://x.com/froessell/status/2110671824279179694); [Substack](https://froessell.substack.com/p/15000-from-one-browser-game-the-math)

### Inferences
- CrazyGames has a looser size limit than Poki (50 MB vs 8 MB) and an open submission funnel with an automated test, so it is likely the easiest first target for a beginner. Earnings start only after passing the metrics gate.

### Gaps
- The exact conversion-to-gameplay benchmark and the official general revenue-share percentage were not found.

## 4. GameDistribution, itch.io, Y8, Newgrounds, Google H5 Games Ads (Ad Placement API)

### Takeaway
GameDistribution syndicates a game to many sites for about 33% of net revenue (from a competitor's claim). itch.io lets creators set the platform's cut (default 10%) and suits paid or pay-what-you-want games, but median earnings are near zero. Y8 pays 50% of in-game ad revenue through your own AdSense account. Google's H5 Games Ads (adBreak/adConfig) allow interstitial and rewarded ads on your own site, but it is a closed beta that requires approval.

### Cited Findings
- GameDistribution: 33% of net revenue, a €100 threshold, and payment within 60 days of the monthly report — [Playgama comparison](https://playgama.com/blog/business-faqs/poki-vs-crazygames-vs-gamedistribution-revenue-share/) (competitor source). Older forum complaints (2019) describe payment problems — [html5gamedevs](https://html5gamedevs.com/topic/32413-gamedistributioncom-offers-revenue-share). SDKs exist for Unity, Construct and plain JS (older) — [Defold forum 2020](https://forum.defold.com/t/gamedistribution-html5-sdk-in-2020/66341)
- Other aggregators: GameMonetize 45% (90% if you also host on your own site), Y8 50% — [impulsemediahub / pixelgamecraft summary](https://www.impulsemediahub.com/blog/html5-game-monetization-2026/); [portalready](https://portalready.world/portals/)
- itch.io open revenue sharing: the creator chooses 0–100% for itch.io, default 10% (since 2015). Payment processors charge about 2.9% + $0.30 — [itch.io update](https://itch.io/updates/introducing-open-revenue-sharing); [cinevva itch guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/itch-io-launch-guide)
- itch.io earnings are "wildly top-heavy"; the median is close to zero, and most paid games earn under $50 a month — [cinevva monetization](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/web-game-monetization)
- Y8: AFP (recommended) connects your own AdSense account, and you earn 50% of in-game ad revenue. A manual invoice-based revenue share also exists. Requires an approved game and a studio. Official JS SDK — [Y8 revshare](https://ar.y8.com/revshare) [primary]; [Y8 AFP SDK GitHub](https://github.com/Y8Games/y8-afp-ads-sdk-javascript); [Y8 newsroom June 2026](https://tr.y8.com/newsroom/y8-indie-developers-global-audience)
- Newgrounds: ad revenue share paid out above $50, with more weight on Supporter membership ($5 a month from April 2026). Unverified, from one secondary source — [cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/web-game-monetization)
- Google H5 Games Ads: any publisher with an H5 games site may apply, but approval is not guaranteed and the program is a closed beta. It grants limited permission for full-screen and rewarded ads. The API uses adBreak() and adConfig(), and Google decides whether an ad shows. Docs updated 2025-07-26 — [AdSense H5 Games Ads](https://adsense.google.com/start/h5-games-ads/); [Ad Placement API beta](https://developers.google.com/ad-placement/docs/beta); [Use the API](https://developers.google.com/ad-placement/apis) [primary]

### Inferences
- Self-hosting with H5 Games Ads only pays if you can bring your own traffic, which is hard for a beginner. Portals provide the traffic, so Poki and CrazyGames come first, with GameDistribution and Y8 as secondary syndication. Exclusivity deals and SDK rules (only the portal's own ads) can restrict multi-portal distribution, so check terms per deal.

### Gaps
- No primary GameDistribution or Newgrounds revenue terms were retrieved.

## 5. Realistic revenue examples and technical/engine requirements

### Takeaway
Real reported numbers are low per play. One developer earned about €1.20 per 1,000 CrazyGames plays (older simple WebGL games). Secondary guides put a well-performing casual portal game at about $200–$2,000 a month, and a good first Poki game at about $500–$3,000 a month (estimates). Top Poki developers make $50k–$1M a year. Meaningful income needs millions of plays or a portfolio of games. Lightweight JS engines fit the size limits best.

### Cited Findings
- €556.92 from 451,327 CrazyGames plays across 8 simple WebGL games (about €1.20 per 1,000 plays), plus $163.79 on Kongregate from 104,444 plays (games from about 2018) — via [pixelgamecraft](https://pixelgamecraft.com/blog/html5-game-income/) / [froessell Substack](https://froessell.substack.com/p/15000-from-one-browser-game-the-math)
- "$15,000 from one browser game takes 1 to 15 million plays" (modeled at $2–$10 eCPM) — [froessell on X](https://x.com/froessell/status/2110671824279179694)
- Rewarded eCPM assumptions: $15–$28 US, $8–$15 EU, $1–$3 tier-3 markets; $8–$12 blended globally — [impulsemediahub](https://www.impulsemediahub.com/blog/html5-game-monetization-2026/) (modeled, not reported)
- A well-performing casual game on Poki or CrazyGames: about $200–$2,000 a month — [cinevva monetization](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/web-game-monetization) (unsourced estimate). A first Poki game: about $500–$3,000 a month, depending on genre and updates — [cinevva Poki](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-poki) (estimate)
- Top Poki developers: $50k–$1M a year — [Dealroom/Poki PR](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/poki-hits-1b-monthly-plays-as-developer-first-model-boosts-top-studio-revenues-tenfold-1)
- An AI-generated game reportedly earned $25,000 (developer Substack; details not verified) — [gameplaydev Substack](https://gameplaydev.substack.com/p/my-ai-generated-game-earned-25000)
- Engine sizes, from Poki's guide: an empty Defold project is about 1.03 MB, Stencyl about 500 KB and GameMaker about 450–550 KB compressed. Unity is the hardest fit for 8 MB (needs a WASM build and optimization) — [Poki web engine guide](https://developers.poki.com/guide/web-engine); [cinevva Poki](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-poki)
- CrazyGames publishes Unity export tips, optimization tips and "getting to the first frame" guidance — [Unity export tips](https://docs.crazygames.com/resources/export-tips); [Optimization tips](https://docs.crazygames.com/resources/optimization-tips/); [First frame](https://docs.crazygames.com/resources/getting-to-the-first-frame/)
- A Three.js game needed changes to get onto CrazyGames (SDK, loading, etc.) — [froessell Substack](https://froessell.substack.com/p/15000-from-one-browser-game-the-math)

### Inferences
- Rough math at about €1–5 per 1,000 plays: 100k plays a month is about €100–500. Reaching about $1,000 a month means roughly 200k–1M plays a month, which requires passing Poki's or CrazyGames' gates and getting promoted.
- For a Claude Code beginner, the recommended stack is Phaser 3/4 (2D) or Three.js (light 3D) with plain JS: small builds, mobile touch support, and fast loading to the first frame. Integrate portal SDK hooks (gameplayStart/Stop, commercialBreak, rewardedBreak) early.

### Gaps
- No verified, first-party Reddit postmortems with 2025–2026 monthly portal revenue for a single game were found. Most numbers are estimates or older data. No Poki-specific guidance on Construct or Phaser was found.
