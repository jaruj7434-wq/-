# Small-Team / Solo Indie Hits on Steam, 2024–2026 (as of 2026-11-09)

Method note: These notes come from web search result summaries. Direct page fetches failed because these domains are blocked in this session's network: windowscentral.com, beacondigitalpr.com, gameworldobserver.com. Valve does not publish sales numbers. Most figures are developer or publisher announcements (more reliable) or third-party estimates from Alinea, VG Insights, Gamalytic, or Boxleiter-style trackers (less reliable). Each figure below is labelled with which kind it is.

## 1. Which small-team indie games were breakout hits in 2024, 2025 and 2026?

### Takeaway
The biggest recent small-team hits share a pattern. They have one instantly readable hook, they are cheap ($5–$20), and they are either highly replayable solo loops (Balatro, Ball x Pit) or chaotic co-op "friendslop" that streamers clip well (R.E.P.O., PEAK, Meccha Chameleon). Several were built very quickly: PEAK took about a one-month jam, and Meccha Chameleon took about 2 months.

### Cited Findings

**2024 releases / hits**
- **Balatro** (solo dev LocalThunk; publisher Playstack). Poker roguelite deckbuilder. Publisher confirmed 5 million units sold, announced at Pocket Gamer Connects London on 20 Jan 2025, less than a year after release. Development began 13 Dec 2021 with a "weird prototype" that had no jokers. The final months were "pretty gruelling." — [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/01/21/balatro-another-1-5-million-copies-total-5m-units); [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/balatro-sells-5-million-copies-after-end-of-year-spike); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/roguelike/balatro-creator-initially-considered-a-steam-release-in-part-to-help-get-a-game-developer-job-somewhere-and-after-5-million-sales-id-say-he-found-one)
- Balatro is cited as the first solo-dev game nominated for Game of the Year. — [Dexerto/search summary](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/steams-best-selling-games-of-2025-reveal-2-indie-hits-that-made-it-big-3298767/)
- **Buckshot Roulette** (solo dev Mike Klubnika; publisher Critical Reflex). Horror gambling / Russian-roulette game with one mechanic. Released on itch.io on 28 Dec 2023, then on Steam on 4 Apr 2024. It passed 1M on Steam in under 2 weeks. The developer later announced 8 million copies on Steam, and console ports slipped to 2026. A Boxleiter-based tracker estimates far lower, at 2.2–4.5M. — [Game Developer press release](https://gamedeveloper.com/press-release/viral-horror-hit-buckshot-roulette-crosses-1-million-copies-sold-on-steam); [Automaton JP](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20240418-290136/); [TechRaptor via OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/news/25244/buckshot-roulette-sales-hit-8m-console-ports-delayed-to-2026-techraptor); contradicted by [SteamPageAnalyzer estimate](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/games/2835570)
- **Content Warning** (Landfall). Co-op horror where you film scares for "SpookTube". It launched with a free-for-a-day giveaway, sold 1M copies in about 2 weeks afterwards, and Landfall later announced 2.2M sold. The claim of "6 million free copies" was found only on a scraped mirror and is unverified. — [Gamasutra/Game Developer](https://gamasutra.com/business/content-warning-sells-1-million-copies-following-launch-giveaway); [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/content-warning-sells-2-2-million-copies-console-release-possibly-coming)
- **Lethal Company** (solo dev Zeekerss). Late-2023 release, so older context. It is the template for the co-op horror "friendslop" wave, with an estimated 10M copies by Jan 2024 (estimate). — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/lethal-company-sold-an-estimated-10-million-copies)
- **Rusty's Retirement** (solo dev Jordan Morris / Mister Morris Games, Unity). Idle farming sim that occupies the bottom or side of the screen while you do other work. It sold 100K in 5 days and 200K in about 2 weeks (2024), and reached about 550K by Jul 2025 per Wikipedia. A tracker estimates about 1M (unconfirmed). The developer is credited with spawning the "games that run at the bottom of your screen" genre. He says he learned to code only 5 years earlier and his 2 games have sold 750K combined. — [GamesRadar 200K](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/management/amid-rave-reviews-and-200000-sales-idle-stardew-valley-style-farming-sim-dev-can-hardly-believe-their-success-im-only-now-realizing-how-life-changing-this-is); [GamesRadar genre](https://gamesradar.com/games/simulation/after-selling-100000-copies-of-its-idle-stardew-valley-like-rustys-retirement-dev-celebrates-spawning-a-genre-of-games-that-run-at-the-bottom-of-your-screen); [GamesRadar 750K / learned to code](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/indie-dev-behind-quietly-massive-steam-trend-says-they-only-learned-to-code-5-years-ago-but-have-made-2-games-selling-750-000-copies-i-still-dont-believe-it-myself-sometimes); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rusty%27s_Retirement); [GameDiscoverCo case study](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-rustys-retirement-idle-farmed)

Alinea estimated 4.7M copies in April 2025 alone, and later estimates are around 8M copies (unverified, no official figure). TVGS later opened an office and expected about 4 staff by year end. — [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/104474/schedule-hits-459k-peak-players-the-most-by-solo-developer-in-steam-history/index.html); [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/walter-white-simulator-schedule-i-becomes-steam-s-most-popular-indie-game-of-2025); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/life-sim/schedule-1-developer-tvgs-is-an-actual-game-studio-now-with-an-office-desks-and-a-new-guy-named-rob-by-the-end-of-the-year-there-will-likely-be-4-people-working-on-schedule-1); [Gaming Amigos (8M)](https://www.gamingamigos.com/post/schedule-1-reportedly-sold-8-million-copies)
- **R.E.P.O.** (semiwork, small team; I could not confirm the exact headcount or price). Physics-based co-op horror where players extract valuables. - **PEAK** (Aggro Crab + Landfall). Co-op climbing "friendslop", launched 17 Jun 2025. It was made in about a month-long jam in a Seoul Airbnb by 3 Aggro Crab and 4 Landfall developers. It sold 1M in 6 days and about 10M in 8 weeks (Alinea; 10M confirmed at Gamescom ONL). By its first anniversary (Jun 2026) it had passed 20M players. It had 250K wishlists at launch, but Devolver said wishlists were "not a strong predictor" of sales. — [Windows Central (via search)](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/ball-x-pit-crosses-1-million-copies-sold-2026-roadmap); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ball_x_Pit); [Destructoid Next Fest](https://www.destructoid.com/ball-x-pit-is-steam-next-fest-demo/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/gaming/the-spellbinding-ball-x-pit-will-hit-pc-and-consoles-on-october-15-141156022.html); [GamesIndustry.biz (Devolver interview)](https://hn.jakechampion.name/reader/https://www.gamesindustry.biz/how-devolvers-focus-on-small-creative-unique-risk-taking-titles-paid-off-with-the-million-selling-ball-x-pit)

**2026 releases / hits**
- **Meccha Chameleon** (Japan; 2-person team lemorion_1224 + Haganeiro, who met in the Fortnite custom-map creator community; one outlet calls it solo). - **How to Fish** (small Canadian studio, Patreon-funded). Reported at about 1M copies within 2 days of Early Access in May 2026 (secondary source). — [Gaming.net](https://www.gaming.net/best-indie-games-on-steam/)

### Inferences
- The highest-volume 2025–2026 hits are cheap co-op party games (R.E.P.O., PEAK, Meccha Chameleon). Their sales scale because one friend's purchase pulls in 2–3 more. Solo-play hits (Balatro, Ball x Pit, Buckshot Roulette, Rusty's Retirement) sell fewer units but still reach life-changing revenue for 1–2 people.
- Short development cycles (1–2 months for PEAK and Meccha Chameleon) suggest that a beginner should build small, quickly testable prototypes rather than a multi-year project. Note, however, that those teams were experienced developers.
- Rusty's Retirement is the most relevant analogue for a beginner: a solo developer who learned to code about 5 years earlier, a low-scope idle design, a clear novel format (screen-edge idle), and about 550K sales.

### Gaps
- Exact team size and price of R.E.P.O. (semiwork) were not verified. Exact team size for Ball x Pit is contested (solo vs. "a few collaborators").
- Vampire Survivors, Brotato, and Halls of Torment are pre-2024 or not researched here, so I have no fresh 2024–2026 sourced figures for them.
- I found no confirmed sales for Bongo Cat-style paid desktop pets or for other 2025–2026 desktop-idle titles, and no title for the €6.15 solo-dev hit.
- Late-2026 (Aug–Nov) breakout hits beyond Meccha Chameleon are not well covered.

## 2. What genres/trends are rising on Steam?

### Takeaway
The clearest rising trends are co-op "friendslop" (physics comedy and horror with proximity chat), idle and desktop-companion games (now an official Steam tag), genre-blend roguelites (deckbuilder or brick-breaker crossed with survivors-like), and cheap simulator/shop-management games.

### Cited Findings
- Co-op games with slapstick physics drove much of the 2025 indie success, with R.E.P.O. and PEAK leading units. — [Game World Observer (Alinea)](https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/12/22/indie-projects-generated-a-quarter-of-the-total-game-revenue-on-steam-by-the-end-of-2025-analytics)
- PEAK "put friendslop on the map in 2025". — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/survival/peak-put-friendslop-on-the-map-in-2025-but-neither-of-its-2-studios-expected-it-to-blow-up-we-were-ready-to-hit-the-launch-button-and-go-into-vacation-mode)
- Meccha Chameleon was described as a "viral co-op friendslop game" that sold more than 10M copies. — [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/news/33920/viral-co-op-friendslop-game-sells-over-10-million-copies-since-june-9)
- Next Fest top demos in 2026 included "gnomeslop" (Burglin' Gnomes), open-world piracy, and vampire card battling. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/steam-next-fests-top-demos-were-packed-with-gnomeslop-hijinks-open-world-piracy-and-vampire-card-battling-but-then-well-marathon-happened)
- "Desktop companion" is now an official Steam tag. — [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/list/5-best-desktop-companion-games-to-try-now-that-its-a-steam-tag-official-genre/)
- Kotaku described a Next Fest wave of games that "mostly play themselves while you do other stuff on your desktop", such as Desktop Defender. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/steam-next-fests-most-unlikely-breakout-only-requires-a-fraction-of-your-screen-and-attention-2000639943)
- Rusty's Retirement is credited with spawning the screen-edge idle genre. — [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/games/simulation/after-selling-100000-copies-of-its-idle-stardew-valley-like-rustys-retirement-dev-celebrates-spawning-a-genre-of-games-that-run-at-the-bottom-of-your-screen)
- Ball x Pit is a "genre blender": brick-breaking plus ball-fusing plus base-building survival roguelite. — [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2025/06/brick-breaking-ball-fusing-base-building-survival-roguelite-game-ball-x-pit-launches-in-2025-for-xbox-series-switch-and-pc); [PCGamesN ("Pong roguelike")](https://www.pcgamesn.com/ball-x-pit/demo-pong-roguelike-steam-next-fest)
- Simulator and shop-management games are strong: Supermarket Simulator, TCG Card Shop Simulator, Schedule I (drug-empire management), and Probably Stolen (shopkeeping). — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/steams-best-selling-games-of-2025-reveal-2-indie-hits-that-made-it-big-3298767/); [GameLevate](https://gamelevate.com/probably-stolen-750000-sales-steam-success/)
- Indie games were about 25% of Steam's 2025 revenue per Alinea, on a platform total of about $17.7B. Sources disagree on that total: about $20B per Alinea vs about $16.9B per GameDiscoverCo, depending on the reference. — [Tech4Gamers](https://tech4gamers.com/25-steams-revenue-in-2025-from-indie-titles/)

### Inferences
- Friendslop is now crowded. Meccha Chameleon succeeded with a fresh, visually readable twist (paint yourself to hide) rather than another Lethal Company clone. A beginner entering co-op needs an equally simple, clip-able hook, and must handle netcode, which is a big technical risk for a beginner.
- Desktop idle / companion games are low-scope and art-light, and AI-assisted art is plausible for them. But Bongo Cat shows that free-to-play with cosmetic drops can draw huge CCU and still produce little revenue. A paid premium model like Rusty's Retirement at a low price looks more monetizable.

### Gaps
- No quantitative tag-level growth data (e.g., GameDiscoverCo genre revenue shares for 2025–2026) could be fetched directly.
- I found no data on auto-battler or incremental-only revenue trends specifically.

## 3. Typical sales distribution reality and Steam fees

### Takeaway
The median Steam game earns very little, roughly $1K–$3.5K lifetime gross by most estimates. Only about 3% of 2025 releases reached 1,000 reviews. The $100 fee is trivial; discovery is the real cost.

### Cited Findings
- Zukowski's analysis of VG Insights data found the median 2019-cohort game earned $1,136 lifetime. A 2026 model of paid games with at least one review gives about $3,482. Other 2025 medians range from about $249 to $2,302, roughly a tenfold disagreement. — [SteamPageAnalyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/indie-game-revenue-data); [SteamPageAnalyzer median 2026](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/median-steam-game-2026)
- Typical indie copies sold are estimated at about 200–400. — [SteamPageAnalyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/indie-game-sales-statistics)
- There were 20,282 Steam releases in 2025, and only 608 reached 1,000 reviews (Zukowski count). — [SteamPageAnalyzer summary of Zukowski](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/indie-game-revenue-data)
- About 11,000 games had been released in 2024 at the time Rusty's Retirement was in the top 50 highest-rated. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/this-is-definitely-my-biggest-life-achievement-idle-stardew-valley-like-rustys-retirement-hits-top-50-highest-rated-steam-games-among-the-11000-released-this-year)
- Boxleiter ratio: the original estimate is about 50 sales per review. GameDiscoverCo's "NB number" (New Boxleiter) averaged about 63 in 2025, and genre ranges of 20–60 are used. — [SteamPageAnalyzer / VoxBooster summaries](https://voxbooster.com/blog/indie-game-statistics-2026/)
- Wishlist-to-sales: one analysis puts the median first-week conversion at about 0.12, versus older rules of thumb of 0.15–0.25. Alinea has a study on wishlist-to-buyer conversion for Next Fest-demo games. — [Game-Oracle](https://www.game-oracle.com/blog/wishlist-to-sales-2025); [Alinea Substack](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/wishlist-to-buyer-conversions-for)
- Zukowski's launch wishlist tiers: under 6K is "Bronze", 6K is "Silver", 30K is "Gold", 150K is "Diamond". — [SteamPageAnalyzer summary](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/indie-game-revenue-data)
- Steam Direct fee: $100 per app, non-refundable but recouped once the app reaches $1,000 adjusted gross revenue (Valve docs). That is roughly 130–150 copies at $9.99 (third-party estimate). — [Steamworks docs](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee); [SteamPageAnalyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/steam-direct-fee-explained)
- Revenue share: 30% up to $10M, 25% from $10M to $50M, 20% above $50M, applied per app on revenue past each threshold. I could not verify these tiers directly on a Steamworks page in this session. — [presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/how-much-does-steam-take); [Fungies](https://fungies.io/how-to-sell-a-game-on-steam)
- Gross is not net: for Schedule I's ~$125–151M gross estimates, commentators note the developer's take is unknown but "well above $50M". — [Gaming Amigos](https://www.gamingamigos.com/post/schedule-1-reportedly-sold-8-million-copies)

### Inferences
- Rough developer net is about 50–60% of headline "gross revenue" estimates, after Valve's 30%, refunds, regional pricing, VAT, and publisher splits where applicable. This is my inference, not a sourced figure.
- A beginner should plan for a median outcome (under $5K) and treat a hit as upside. The practical target is reaching 6K–30K wishlists before launch.

### Gaps
- Primary GameDiscoverCo / How To Market A Game pages could not be fetched; figures above are via secondary summaries.

## 4. Common success factors: price, session length, replayability, streamer virality, demos, Next Fest

### Takeaway
Hits combine a cheap price ($5–$15), a hook you understand in a 10-second clip, short repeatable sessions or co-op chaos, and a strong pre-launch demo or Next Fest presence. Wishlist momentum before Next Fest is the strongest predictor of Next Fest results.

### Cited Findings
- **Price:** Meccha Chameleon $4.79–$5.99; Ball x Pit $14.99; a €6.15 solo game made its developer a millionaire in a week. — [Hitmarker](https://hitmarker.net/news/indie-hit-meccha-chameleon-is-now-the-best-selling-game-of-2026-1944965); [SteamDB / PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/ball-x-pit/demo-pong-roguelike-steam-next-fest); [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/solo-developer-sells-his-game-for-e6-15-on-steam-becomes-a-millionaire-within-a-week,1573205/)
- **Streamer / clip virality:** Meccha Chameleon grew via word of mouth and viral Twitch clips with no marketing budget. — [Hitmarker](https://hitmarker.net/news/indie-hit-meccha-chameleon-is-now-the-best-selling-game-of-2026-1944965)
- **Free giveaway as a viral launch:** Content Warning was free for a day at launch, then sold 1M in about 2 weeks. — [Gamasutra](https://gamasutra.com/business/content-warning-sells-1-million-copies-following-launch-giveaway)
- **itch.io-first:** Buckshot Roulette was viral on itch.io (Dec 2023) before its Steam launch (Apr 2024). — [OpenCritic/TechRaptor](https://opencritic.com/news/25244/buckshot-roulette-sales-hit-8m-console-ports-delayed-to-2026-techraptor)

## 5. Steam's AI-generated content disclosure policy and player reaction

### Takeaway
AI use is allowed on Steam but must be disclosed when the AI content ships with the game and players consume it. Disclosure is now common (about 20% of 2025 releases), but the store page shows it and some players filter it out. High-profile backlash cases exist, so AI-generated art carries a reputational risk.

### Cited Findings
- The disclosure requirement has existed since 2024 and appears in an "AI Generated Content Disclosure" store-page section. Valve later "significantly" rewrote the rules: developers flag generative content only when it "ships with your game, and is consumed by players". The rules separate pre-generated content from live-generated content, and an overlay lets players report violating AI content. — [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/valve-has-significantly-rewritten-steams-rules-for-how-developers-much-disclose-ai-use)
- In July 2025, 7,818 of about 114K Steam games (about 7%) had AI disclosures. About 20–21% of 2025 releases disclosed AI, up roughly 700–800% year on year. — [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/pc-gaming/1-in-5-steam-games-released-in-2025-use-generative-ai-up-nearly-700-percent-year-on-year-7-818-titles-disclose-genai-asset-usage-7-percent-of-entire-steam-library); [VGC 800%](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/steam-games-disclosing-generative-ai-use-are-up-800-this-year/)
- Only 8 of Steam's 2025 top sellers disclosed generative AI use (headline claim). — [RaiderKing](https://raiderking.com/only-8-of-steams-2025-top-sellers-disclosed-generative-ai-use/)
- Backlash cases: Jurassic World Evolution 3 removed AI-generated portraits after backlash, and The Alters was criticized for not initially disclosing AI-generated text. — [search summary of multiple outlets; see VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/valve-has-significantly-rewritten-steams-rules-for-how-developers-much-disclose-ai-use)
- One analysis claims AI labels carry a measurable commercial penalty (single source, treat cautiously). — [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/ai-content-labels-killing-game-sales/)
- Tim Sweeney (Epic) argued against the labels, saying "AI will be involved in nearly all future production". — [search summary; see VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/valve-has-significantly-rewritten-steams-rules-for-how-developers-much-disclose-ai-use)
- Steam has no native filter to hide AI games, but SteamDB added a filter for AI-disclosed games. — [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/pc-gaming/1-in-5-steam-games-released-in-2025-use-generative-ai-up-nearly-700-percent-year-on-year-7-818-titles-disclose-genai-asset-usage-7-percent-of-entire-steam-library)

### Inferences
- A beginner using Higgsfield for in-game art or audio must disclose it. Since few top sellers disclose AI, the safest options are to use AI mainly for marketing materials or prototyping, use a deliberately stylized or simple art direction, or hand-edit assets. Code written with Claude Code that does not ship as player-consumed generated content appears to fall outside the "consumed by players" clause under the revised rules. That reading is mine, not Valve's wording; check the current Steamworks text.
- Genres where art matters less (abstract, minimalist, UI-heavy idle, card or number games like Balatro) reduce both AI dependence and backlash risk.

### Gaps
- No rigorous controlled data on the sales impact of AI disclosure was found; evidence is anecdotal or from single analyses.
- I did not fetch the exact current Steamworks AI-disclosure page text.
